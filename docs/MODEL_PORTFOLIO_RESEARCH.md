# Choosing a model for the workload

Reviewed November 25, 2026. The initial 40-entry selection included 38 MIT or Apache-2.0 releases and the two Gemma 3 vision checkpoints served by the engine under Google's Gemma terms. Each upstream entry records its license, size, integration path, a dated adoption count and evidence for a specific use case. Within that initial selection, five evaluations came from independent teams and 35 from model publishers. Evidence ranges from multi-dataset benchmarks to one qualitative example for MOSS-Image-SFT. The expanded catalog also includes custom, gated and research-licensed releases. Build records give exact quantizations and artifact sizes. [Browse the portfolio](MODEL_PORTFOLIO.md)

## What ran locally

**Tabular classification.** On scikit-learn's Wine dataset (178 rows, 13 numeric features, three classes), we used five shuffled stratified folds with split seed 42. Every prediction came from a model fitted without that row. The CPU run used the pinned Mitra 2 checkpoint, AutoGluon 1.6.3, one estimator and no fine-tuning. The baseline was a fold-fitted StandardScaler and logistic regression with `max_iter=1000` in scikit-learn 1.9.1. Metrics pool all held-out predictions. The difference was one sample on one small dataset. [Dataset description](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.datasets.load_wine.html)

| Model | Correct | Accuracy | Macro F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mitra 2 | 176/178 | 98.88% | 0.9897 |
| Logistic regression | 175/178 | 98.31% | 0.9826 |

**English passage reranking.** We scored three hand-written queries with the pinned MiniLM-L6 cross-encoder, CPU float32, Transformers 5.24.1 and PyTorch 2.10.0. Each query had a relevant answer, a distractor repeating words from the query, and an unrelated passage. The relevant answer beat the unrelated passage in all three cases. It ranked first only for the hinge question. These deliberately difficult examples show a failure mode and are too few to estimate traffic accuracy.

| Query | Correct answer and raw logit | Keyword-matching distractor and raw logit |
|---|---|---|
| How do I keep a cut avocado from turning brown? | “Brush the exposed flesh with lemon juice and refrigerate it in an airtight container.” −10.1100 | “The avocado tree turns brown in winter when its leaves dry out.” +3.2347 |
| How can I stop a door hinge from squeaking? | “Apply a few drops of lubricant to the hinge pin and swing the door back and forth.” −0.7236 | “The door hinge showroom displays antique hinges and decorative door handles.” −7.7467 |
| What causes the ocean tides to rise and fall? | “The gravitational attraction of the Moon and, to a lesser extent, the Sun creates the periodic change in sea level.” −5.7702 | “The ocean tides calendar lists the rise and fall times for every harbor.” +3.1257 |

Both runs used the model libraries directly. Service availability is reported separately by the deployment's `GET /v1/models` endpoint.

## How candidates were selected

We sampled the ten most-downloaded Hub repositories in each of 12 pipeline categories. The categories covered text generation, tabular and zero-shot classification, sentence similarity, feature extraction, text ranking, image-to-text, speech recognition, text-to-speech, text-to-image, image-to-image and forecasting. We checked candidates against upstream model cards, technical papers and benchmark releases, including lower-volume models that supplied missing capabilities. The ranked search selected Apache-2.0 and MIT checkpoints. Gemma 3 4B and 12B were added separately to track builds for the existing vision service. [Example ranked query](https://huggingface.co/api/models?pipeline_tag=text-ranking&sort=downloads&direction=-1&limit=10)

The snapshot reads the Hub's `downloads` field from `/api/models/{publisher}/{repository}` for each included artifact. Hub counts measure downloads over the preceding 30 days, using library-specific file-counting rules. Automated fetches, caching and conversions affect counts. For example, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 recorded 164.73M downloads, the MS MARCO MiniLM reranker 54.15M, BGE-M3 35.33M, Florence-2 base 23.65M and Kokoro 13.74M. The repository ID fixes which artifact gets credit. [Hub counting methodology](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/models-download-stats)

Three candidates illustrate the limits of using that list alone. GPT-2 led the text-generation sample with 32.89M downloads, but its base language-model objective does not address the instruction-following use case. The 12-layer MiniLM reranker gained 0.01 MRR@10 points over the six-layer checkpoint on the cited test. AutoGluon's Chronos-2 distribution counted 49.53M downloads while Amazon's repository counted 9.71M. We list the upstream checkpoint once and use Amazon's count.
